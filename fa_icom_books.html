📖 How to Download and Use These AIOU Original Books

  • Click the “PDF” link next to your course code. The file will open in a new tab or download directly.
  • Save the file to your phone, tablet, or computer. Create a folder named “AIOU FA ICom Books”.
  • Use a good PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat, Xodo, or Google Drive) for highlighting and bookmarking.
  • Follow the chapter sequence – do not skip any unit, as exams cover the full syllabus.
  • Solve all exercise questions – they are often repeated in assignments and final exams.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are these the same books that AIOU sends by courier?

Yes, these are exact digital copies of the physical textbooks provided by AIOU. You can start studying immediately while waiting for the printed books to arrive.

What is the difference between FA and I.Com?

FA (Faculty of Arts) focuses on humanities subjects like Economics, Sociology, History, Psychology, etc. I.Com (Intermediate in Commerce) focuses on accounting, business math, banking, and management. Both are 2‑year intermediate programs.

Can I print these PDFs?

Absolutely. You can take a printout for personal use. Use high‑quality paper and bind the pages if you prefer a hard copy.
