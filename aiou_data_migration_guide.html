🔍 Scenario Matrix: Aapko Data Migration Kab Zaroori Hai?

🎓 1. Program-to-Program Leap

Jab aap graduation (e.g. BA/B.Com) complete karne ke baad naye BS Program me admission lete hain aur older elective course code ke Credit Hours transfer karwa kar semesters short karna chahte hon.

⏳ 2. Gap Year Record Sync

Agar aapne university se 2 ya 3 saal tak semesters skip (gap year) kiye hain aur re-admission lene par purane semesters ka record CMS grade card par load nahi ho raha ho.

⚠️ 3. Double ID Allocation

System glitch ya duplicate details ki wajah se AIOU technical center jab aik hi student ke naam par 2 mukhtalif enrollment number/IDs allot kar de, jis se result split ho jata hai.

📍 4. Regional Territory Shift

Jab aap apna permanent address aik city se dusri city (e.g. Lahore to Karachi) shift karte hain aur physical papers ke marks aor assignment credits naye regional center me forward karne hon.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Procedure: Data Migration Online & Offline

Option A: Online Migration (Fastest Way via CMS Portal)

AIOU online service request ke zariye system records merge karna sab se aasan aor safe rasta hai:

  1. AIOU ke official CMS Portal (enrollment.aiou.edu.pk) par apne User ID aur Password ke sath login karein.
  2. Dashboard par maujood "Academic Records" ya "Service Request" icon par click karein.
  3. Create New Request par tap kar ke category select karein: "Data Migration / Record Transfer".
  4. Challan Generation portal par ja kar migration fee (PKR 1000 ya 2000 urgent) ka online challan generate karein. Fee aap EasyPaisa, JazzCash ya kisi bhi Commercial Bank me pay kar sakte hain.
  5. Paid Challan ki copy aor academic certificates ko service portal par attach kar ke request submit kar dein. 5 se 7 working days me check update ho jayega.
⚠️ Online Status Notice: Request submit karne ke baad daily basis par "Service Request Tracker" lazmi check karein. Agar university kisi document par objection lagati hai, toh uska reply 78 hours me submit karna mandatory hai warna request auto-cancel ho jayegi.

Option B: Offline Migration (Postal Dispatch Method)

Agar aapko internet blockages ya online portal par access ka masla pesh aa raha hai, to aap physical registration documents postal mail ke zariye bhi forward kar sakte hain:

  • Upar diye gaye link se official **AIOU Data Migration Request Form** ka print out nikaal kar usey manually capital letters me fill-up karein.
  • AIOU main website ke online Challan Generator engine par ja kar Challan form print karein aur bank branch me direct fee jama karwayein.
  • Apne pichle semesters ki Result Sheet (Official Transcript), CNIC ya B-Form copy aur paid challan form application ke sath staple karein.
  • Envelop par wazeh tor par "Data Migration Section" likhein aur ise AIOU Main Campus, **Directorate of Admissions and Mailing, Block No. 4, H-8, Islamabad** par Pakistan Post Registered Service ke zariye rawana karein.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Data Migration Support Desk)

📌 Meray BA ke credit hours BS 4-Year program me kese transfer hon ge?
Aapko "Option B" ke mutabiq migration form aor online challan bank me pay kar ke admissions block rawana karna ho ga. AIOU BA ke sirf wahi elective courses transfer karti hai jo naye BS syllabus curriculum se minimum 80% course content match karte hain.
📌 Agar application post karne ke baad bhi status change na ho to kya karein?
Is soorat me registry tracking slip ko check karein ke documents main campus block receive ho chuke hain ya nahi. Agar deliver ho chuke hon to AIOU central helpline (051-111-112-468) par query forward karein ya local region office se physical verify karwayein.
📌 Kya normal challan payment EasyPaisa se ho sakti hai?
Bilkul! AIOU ke tamam generated online challans (Admissions, Migration, Re-checking) EasyPaisa, JazzCash aur Upaisa mobile apps ke "Education Payment" feature ke through asani se paid kiye ja sakte hain.
📌 Double enrollment number band hone par purani assignment bach jaye gi?
Haan, merge service request check hone par IT department dono systems ka physical databases data sync kar ke aik unified student identity (primary login code) create kar deta hai, jis se uploaded assignment submissions aor overall final performance aggregate me direct marks update ho jate hain.
