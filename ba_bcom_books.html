What is the difference between BA and B.Com?

BA (Bachelor of Arts) focuses on humanities subjects like Economics, Sociology, History, Psychology, etc. B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce) focuses on accounting, business math, banking, and management. Both are 2‑year or 4‑year programs depending on the scheme.
