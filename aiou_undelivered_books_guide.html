📌 When to Apply for Undelivered Books? (Zaroori Haalaat)

Aapko darj-zail sooraton me is application form ko fill kar ke main mailing block ko rawana karna chahiye:

  • ✅ **Portal Dispatch Date Limit:** CMS portal par dispatch status update hue **45 din** guzar chuke hon aur parcel na mila ho.
  • ✅ **Courier Return Glitch:** Postman ya local courier partner parcel ko "Address incomplete" ya "No user found" likh kar wapis Islamabad head office bhej chuka ho.
  • ✅ **Address Migration:** Aapne haal hi me permanent address shift kiya ho aur purane address par kitabein na mil saki hon.
  • ✅ **Partial Delivery:** Set me se chand code books receive hui hon jabki baki codes gayab hon.
  • ✅ **Delivered Status Mismatch:** Portal par "Delivered" likha aa raha ho lekin physically aapko aor aapki family me kisi ko parcel receive na hua ho.
Application Send Karne Ki Aakhri Tareeq: Books dispatch date se **60 din** ke andar apply karein. 60 din ke baad university free resending accept nahi karti aur replacement late fee deposit karni parti hai.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure to File Undelivered Books Form

  1. Get Print of Application Form: Upar diye gaye central green button par click kar ke official PDF download karein aur iska print out nikalwayin.
  2. Fill Student Particulars:
    • Apna Mukammal Naam, CNIC, Enrollment/Student Registration ID Number, aor phone number safely likhein.
    • Program detail (e.g. BS, B.Ed, BA, FA) aor current Semester detail enter karein.
    • **Missing Book Codes:** Un course codes aor names ki list banayein jin ki books receive nahi huin.
    • **Reason Box:** Kitabein na milne ki waja likhein (jaise postal service failure, home locked or address change).
  3. Assemble Verification Documents:
    • Attested copy of CNIC or B-Form.
    • AIOU paid admission fee challan copy.
    • **CMS Books Tracking Screen Print:** Apne CMS portal me ja kar "Books Dispatch tracking status" ka clear screenshot print karein.
    • **Correct/Updated Address Proof:** Utility bill ki copy ya CNIC copy taake correct address register ho sake.
  4. Assess Fee (Challan Criteria):
    • **University error or postal lost:** Muft (Rs. 0/-).
    • **Student wrong address entry error:** Rs. 200/- to Rs. 500/- processing aor delivery fee. Detail niche tabular guide me shamil hai.
  5. Postal Submission:
    • Apni complete physical file ko envelope me pack kar ke official mail address par registered post rawana karein: **Director of Books & Publications, Block No. 28, Admissions and Mailing Block, AIOU Sector H-8, Islamabad – 44000**.
Pro Student Hack (Regional Office Support): Agar aap H-8 Main Campus, Islamabad ke qareeb rehte hain, to postal delay se bachne ke liye ye file direct physically exam books block window par submit karein. Documents verify hote hi aapko wahan se **By-Hand** kitabein usi waqt issue kar di jati hain!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Undelivered Books Support Desk

🔹 Undelivered application processing ke baad kitabein kab tak milti hain?
Application verify hone ke baad normal schedule me 15 se 30 working days lagte hain. Agar aap by-hand collect karna chahein to Islamabad main head campus se usi din mil jati hain.
🔹 Kya main online check kar sakta hoon ke ghalat address ki waja se books return hui hain ya nahi?
Jee haan! Apne CMS account me "Books Dispatch Status" check karein, agar wahan "Returned" ya "Undelivered" status araha hai to iska matlab hai parcel Islamabad wapis ja chuka hai.
🔹 Kya courier lost hone par fee pay karni ho gi?
Nahi, agar track history me show ho jaye ke parcel courier center ne lost kiya hai to AIOU standard free re-dispatch policy ke mutabiq new book bundle print kar ke free of cost rawana karti hai.
🔹 Kya undelivered form regional office me submit kar sakte hain?
Jee haan! Har city ka AIOU Regional Center ye application receive kar ke centralized admissions and books block ko forward karta hai. Lekin courier direct head office bhejne se processing tez hoti hai.
