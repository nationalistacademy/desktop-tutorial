📖 How to Download and Use These AIOU Original Books

  • Click the Download button next to your course code. The PDF will open in a new tab or download directly.
  • Save the file to your phone, tablet, or computer. Create a folder named “AIOU Books” for easy access.
  • Use a good PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat, Xodo, or Google Drive) for highlighting and bookmarking important pages.
  • Follow the chapter sequence as given in the book. Do not skip any unit – exams cover the full syllabus.
  • Solve all exercise questions – they are often repeated in assignments and final exams.

📚 Key Books (Helping Books) – What You Need to Know

Key books (also called helping books or guides) provide solved assignments, chapter summaries, and additional practice questions. In the table above, most codes have Available, meaning a key book is published by local publishers (Caravan, Dogar, Ilmi, etc.). For code 213 (Sindhi Compulsory), no key book is available – you must rely entirely on the original textbook.

Where to buy key books? You can find them at Urdu Bazaar (Lahore), Saddar (Karachi), or online at Daraz.pk. We also provide free solved assignments for many courses on our solved assignments page.

⚠️ Important: Key books are for reference only. Never copy answers directly from a key book – AIOU uses plagiarism detection. Always read the original book first, then use the key book to check your understanding.

🎯 Tips to Score High Marks Using Original Books + Key Books

  • ✅ Read the original textbook chapter thoroughly before looking at any key book.
  • ✅ Make your own notes – writing helps memory.
  • ✅ Use the key book only to verify your answers or get extra examples.
  • ✅ For practical subjects (lab techniques, garment making, poultry farming), focus on diagrams and step‑by‑step procedures.
  • ✅ Practice past papers available in key books – at least 5 years of past papers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are these the same books that AIOU sends by courier?

Yes, these are exact digital copies of the physical textbooks provided by AIOU. You can start studying immediately while waiting for the printed books to arrive.

Can I print these PDFs?

Absolutely. You can take a printout for personal use. Use high‑quality paper and bind the pages if you prefer a hard copy.

Why is code 213 (Sindhi Compulsory) key book not available?

Some subjects, especially regional languages, have very few helping books published. For such courses, you must rely entirely on the original AIOU textbook and your tutor’s guidance.

How can I get solved assignments for these courses?

Visit our solved assignments page. We cover most codes from 200 to 260. If your code is missing, contact us via WhatsApp.

Are these books valid for the 2026 session?

Yes, AIOU rarely changes these foundational books. The editions listed are the most recent and cover both Spring and Autumn 2026 semesters.

What is the difference between code 256 and 257 (both Fruit Production)?

They may be different parts or semesters of the same subject. Check your admission form or ask your tutor which one applies to you. Both books are provided here for reference.
