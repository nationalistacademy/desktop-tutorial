📌 How to Submit AIOU Associate Degree Assignments?

  • 1. Download the solved assignment PDF for your subject.
  • 2. Read and understand the answers thoroughly.
  • 3. Rewrite the answers in your own handwriting on A4 size ruled papers.
  • 4. Attach the AIOU assignment form (provided in your admission package) as the first page.
  • 5. Submit the assignment to your nearest AIOU regional office or via courier before the deadline.
  • 6. Keep a photocopy or scan for future reference.
⚠️ Pro Tip: Never copy the solved assignment exactly as given. Paraphrase, add your own examples, and present neatly. This increases your chances of getting higher marks.
