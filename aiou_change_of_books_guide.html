📌 When is Change of Books Required? (Tabdeeli e Nisab)

Aap darj-zail scenarios me AIOU Books Dispatch Section se subjects replacement ki request kar sakte hain:

  • ✅ **Damaged Print Sheets:** Kitabon ke safhaat phate hue hon, printing text missing ho ya misprinted copies hon.
  • ✅ **Syllabus Change/Wrong Subjects:** Aapke admission form me select kiye gaye codes ke bajaye koi dusre ghalat codes bhej diye gaye hon.
  • ✅ **Voluntary Program Correction:** Program shift (jaise BA Associate Degree se BS program update) hone ki wajah se naye course code materials swap karne hon.
  • ✅ **Language Medium Shift:** Urdu medium books ko English medium curriculum materials se swap karwana ho.
Application Send Karne Ki Aakhri Tareeq: Kitabein receive hone ke **30 din** ke andar hi change of books form bhej dena zaroori hai. 30 din ke baad university is change ko accept nahi karti aor re-postage fees fine surcharge karti hai.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure to Swap/Change AIOU Books

  1. Download Word Form: Upar diye gaye central green button par click kar ke raw Word format document download karein aur print out nikalwayin.
  2. Provide Accurate Student Info:
    • Apna Mukammal Naam, CNIC, Phone Number, and Student ID/Enrollment Number enter karein.
    • Current Semester aur target Program detail (e.g. BS, B.Ed, BA, FA) confirm likhein.
    • **Purani/Ghalat Kitabein:** Un course codes aor titles ki list banayein jo aap ke paas ghalat receive hui hain.
    • **Nayi Required Kitabein:** Sahi course codes aor books ke naam likhein jo aap padhna chahte hain (LMS record se codes confirm kar ke likhein).
  3. Gather Legitimate Verification Records:
    • Attested copy of Student CNIC / B-Form.
    • Paid semester admission fee Challan or receipt details.
    • **Damaged Kitabon ka Proof:** Agar kitabein kharab hain to damaged print pages ki physical copies attach karein.
    • **Program Switch Proof:** Agar admission team ne program change letter issue kiya hai to iski copy zaroor lagayein.
  4. Submit Processing Fee (If Applicable):
    • **AIOU Central Error:** Rs. 0/- (Free replacement).
    • **Student Choice Change:** Rs. 200/- to Rs. 500/- fee bank challan ke through pay karni hogi (Tabular grid details niche shamil hain).
  5. Courier Package Registry:
    • Apni complete file ko packing slip ya courier slip ke sath is location par rawana karein: **Director of Books & Publications, Mailing and Dispatch Cell, Block No. 28, H-8, AIOU Main Campus, Islamabad – 44000**.
Pro Tip for Voluntarily Change of Books: Agar aap voluntary codes change karwana chahte hain to payed challan receipt ke sath **LMS Print** lazmi bhein kyunki agar LMS par updates na huin to change of books accept hone ke bawajood assignments submission me code failure show hota rahe ga!

📋 Required Documents Checklist

  • ✅ Fully filled AIOU Change of Books DOCX Form (downloaded above)
  • ✅ Attested copy of CNIC or student registration slip

  • ✅ Official Program Change Approval Letter (if applicable)
  • ✅ Original paid bank challan for processing fee (if student choice change)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Change of Books Support Desk

🔹 Ghalat kitabein milne ke baad replacement rawana hone me kitna time lagta hai?
Agar error main packaging block ka ho to application approve hone ke 15 se 30 working days ke andar standard post ke zariye new books deliver kar di jati hain.
🔹 Agar main khud Islamabad head office chala jaon to kya book swap foran ho jaye ga?
Jee haan! Block 28 (Books and Publications) main head campus Islamabad par physical counter maujood hai jahan direct documents processing kar ke usi din change card issue kar ke kitabein de di jati hain.
🔹 Kya ghalat selected code ki assignments bhejni parein gi agar books process me hon?
Nahi, jab tak books update na ho jayein tab tak aor jab tak LMS code changes approval na mil jaye, tab tak ghalat assignments submit karne ki zaroorat nahi hai.
🔹 Lost ho chuki kitabon ke replacement ka kya tareeqa hai?
AIOU lost/misplaced books ko replace nahi karti. Lekin aap main campus bookstore se self-payment par book rates ke mutabiq unhein direct purchase kar sakte hain.
